Privacy Policy – Break The Shell

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy explains how Break The Shell (the “Game”), made by Alfish Games, handles information. In short: the Game does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The Game does not ask for, collect or transmit personal information such as your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or device identifiers. It has no user accounts.

Information stored on your device

To work, the Game saves a few things only on your own device: the level you have reached, which tutorials you have seen, and your settings (music and sound effects). This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

The Game shows no ads and contains no analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs. We do not sell or share any data with third parties.

Permissions

The Game only uses the vibration permission, to give haptic feedback while you play. It does not need internet access to be played.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
