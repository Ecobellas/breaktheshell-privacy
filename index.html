Privacy Policy – Break The Shell

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy explains how Break The Shell (the “Game”), made by Alfish Games, handles information. In short: the Game does not ask for any personal information; the Unity game engine it is built with collects technical device and usage information for analytics.

Information we collect

The Game does not ask you for personal information such as your name, email address, contacts, precise location or photos, and it has no user accounts.

The Game is built with the Unity engine (Unity Technologies). When the Game runs, the engine may automatically collect and send to Unity:

  • Device or other identifiers, such as a device or installation identifier;
  • Approximate location, such as the country, derived from your IP address;
  • App interactions, such as when the Game is started and how long it is played;
  • Crash logs, diagnostics and other performance data, such as the device model, operating system version, hardware details and frame rate.

This information is used for analytics: to understand which devices the Game runs on, to fix crashes and to improve its performance. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not linked to your name or contact details. It is sent over an encrypted connection. See Unity's privacy policy for how Unity handles this information.

Information stored on your device

To work, the Game saves a few things only on your own device: the level you have reached, which tutorials you have seen, and your settings (music and sound effects). This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

The Game shows no ads and contains no advertising or tracking SDKs. Apart from the engine's information described above, which Unity processes as our service provider, we do not sell or share any data with third parties.

Permissions

The Game uses the vibration permission to give haptic feedback while you play, and network access, which the engine uses to send the information described above. The Game does not need an internet connection to be played.

Your choices

You can stop all of this by uninstalling the Game. For questions about your data, or to ask for it to be deleted, contact us at the address below.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
