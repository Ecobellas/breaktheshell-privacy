Privacy Policy – Break The Shell

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy explains how Break The Shell (the “Game”), made by Alfish Games, handles information. In short: the Game does not ask for any personal information, and the only data that leaves your device is technical device information collected by the Unity game engine for analytics.

Information we collect

The Game does not ask for, collect or transmit personal information such as your name, email address, contacts, location or photos. It has no user accounts.

The Game is built with the Unity engine (Unity Technologies). When the Game runs, the engine may send Unity technical information about your device, such as a device or installation identifier, the device model, operating system version and hardware details. This is used for analytics: to understand which devices the Game runs on and to improve its performance. It is not used for advertising and is not linked to your identity. See Unity's privacy policy for how Unity handles this information.

Information stored on your device

To work, the Game saves a few things only on your own device: the level you have reached, which tutorials you have seen, and your settings (music and sound effects). This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

The Game shows no ads and contains no advertising or tracking SDKs. Apart from the engine's device information described above, which Unity processes as our service provider, we do not sell or share any data with third parties.

Permissions

The Game only uses the vibration permission, to give haptic feedback while you play. It does not need internet access to be played; when it is online, the engine may send the device information described above.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
